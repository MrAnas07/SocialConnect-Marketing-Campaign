# SocialConnect-Marketing-Campaign

## 📌 Project Overview
This project was created as part of the Digital Marketing Internship at DevelopersHub Corporation. The campaign focuses on promoting a fictional social networking app called **Social Connect** through different digital marketing strategies and content creation activities.

The project includes:
- Market Research
- Campaign Planning
- Social Media Content Creation
- Ad Creatives
- Campaign Performance Analysis
- Final Presentation & Report

---

# 🎯 Campaign Objective
The objective of this campaign is to increase brand awareness and audience engagement for the Social Connect app by using social media marketing strategies and creative promotional content.
